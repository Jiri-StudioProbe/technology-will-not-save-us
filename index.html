Technology will not save us.

A photo exhibition of machines, mid-failure.

Opening hours: whenever the Wi-Fi allows.

Scroll (if your mouse still works)

For years I have been photographing technology at its least dignified. The self-checkout that wants a word with you. The kiosk that rebooted in front of a queue. The smart thing that was, briefly, not. I never meant to collect them. They just kept happening. This is a small, free exhibition of those moments. No venue, no curator, no tickets. Just a link, which, with luck, still works.
