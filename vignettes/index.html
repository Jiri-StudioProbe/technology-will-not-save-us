Transition vignettes

Rough, one transition each, placeholders only. Tiles auto-play live. Click a card to open it full-size: click/space next, ← back, A toggle auto-play, Esc back here. Refer to them by ID, e.g. “P06 for the TV room, I04 between photos”.

Page / room transitions (P)

P01 Curtain bars
Six bars drop, the room swaps behind them, bars lift. Punchy, classic.
P02 Iris from the click
New room opens as a circle from wherever you clicked (random point when auto-playing).
P03 Tile pop
60 squares pop in at random, swap behind them, pop out. Retro / pixel-y.
P04 Push + parallax
New room shoves the old one off sideways; the old one dims and lags behind.
P05 Zoom through
Dive into the old room (toward the click), the new one rushes in from far away.
P06 CRT off / on
Old room collapses to a line then a dot; new one switches on. Natural fit for the TV room.
P07 Glitch tear
Strips of the new room tear through an RGB-split, jittering old one.
P08 Error-window cascade
Win95-style error boxes carpet the screen, room swaps behind, boxes get dismissed. Very on-theme.
P09 Buffering… 99%
Old room greys out, spinner counts to 99%, sticks there for a while, hard cut. The delay is the joke.
P10 Diagonal wipe
Slanted edge sweeps across, old room drifts the other way. Direction follows next / back.

Image / photo transitions (I)

I01 Open from grid
Click a thumbnail: it grows to fill the frame, others recede. Click again to go back. (Hand-rolled FLIP.)
I02 Slices
Next photo arrives as 8 vertical strips sliding in from alternate directions.
I03 Pixelate
Photo pixelates down in power-of-two steps, swaps, resolves back up. Canvas, chunky and retro.
I04 Darkroom develop
Lights go out, red safelight, the next photo develops up out of blur and grey. Slow on purpose.
I05 Shutter + flash
Shutter blades snap shut, white flash, new photo as the flash fades. Quick, photographic.
I06 Toss the print
Top print gets flicked off to the side with a spin; next one settles underneath. Photos-on-a-table feel.
I07 Mask reveal, counter-scaled
New photo wipes up through a mask while its content scales down inside it. The 'premium editorial' one.
I08 Print flip
Photo turns edge-on (3D), the next one turns up from the other side. Like turning a print over.
I09 Rack focus
Old photo defocuses while the new one pulls into focus. Gentle, cinematic, almost no motion.
